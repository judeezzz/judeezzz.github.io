How I Approach Quality

  • Risk first. Focus testing effort where financial, regulatory and customer impact is highest.
  • Shift left. Bring testers into refinement early and make requirements testable before code is written.
  • Automate the right things. A balanced test pyramid — fast unit and API checks, focused UI journeys.
  • Measure and improve. Track defect leakage, automation coverage and pipeline health to guide decisions.
  • Share knowledge. Mentoring, technical writing and building a quality culture across teams.
